# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Flutter/Dart chosen by user. Windows and Android first; native integrations follow feasibility tests.

## Users and purpose

Compii helps people learn software, research questions and complete computer tasks. Target adult learners and creators is a planning assumption. The user approved the six-document planning baseline in `docs/compii/` and asked to proceed.

## Brand commitments

Compii, organic lilac Wisp, large white eyes, no robotic features. Calm, curious, quietly witty. User chose the concept at `docs/compii/assets/compii-wisp-concept.png` and explicitly chose Face View as phone default, with optional Full-body View that floats or rests. Preserve this direction; do not restart concept selection.

## Capabilities and constraints

Teach me, With me and For me. Multiple model providers. Desktop works without a phone. The first implementation slice is a local visual companion and preference persistence; it must not imply working AI, recording, device pairing or computer control.

## Evidence

Original Swift Bluey implementation and six planning documents. Generated concept is a design reference, not a functioning application screenshot.

## Accessibility

Keyboard access, labelled controls, contrast, text scaling and reduced motion. Native permissions and connectivity cannot be faked.
