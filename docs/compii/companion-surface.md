# Companion surface

Mode: Operate. Extends the user-approved Wisp identity. This is a code-rendered native application, not a reproduction of the concept board's illustrated monitor and desk.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the two presentations tangible and switchable before connecting intelligence.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory workspace, plum stage, lilac organic character, large white eyes; Material controls with system typography as explicitly permitted by the design baseline.

STORY: Meet Wisp, choose a presentation, explore expressions, retain the preference. Clearly identify local preview state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wisp occupies the main stage. A compact appearance inspector sits beside it on desktop and below it on narrow screens. Full-screen phone preview uses the same renderer.

INTERACTION: Face/full-body switching preserves expression; Float/Rest applies only to full body. Reduced motion stops drift. Gaze responds gently to pointer position.

QUALITY: No false listening or connected indicators; no decorative fake chat history. Layout scrolls under text scaling. Persistence failures are visible and do not silently overwrite stored preferences.
